## Project Insights
The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of a goat production system.
From the analysis, Several key insights were identified:
* Feed Costs Represent The Largest Expense
* Total Farm Expenditure Is High Relative To Feed Cost
* WAD Breed Shows Strong Financial Activity
* Expenses Fluctuate Over Time
* Concentrate and Grass are Major Feed Components

## Final Conclusion
This Goat Farming Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of farm operations by integrating key performance indicators related to goat population, feed consumption, feed costs, and overall expenditures. The analysis reveals that feed constitutes a major component of production expenses, while expenditure patterns vary across breeds and operational periods. The WAD breed demonstrates the highest financial allocation, suggesting its importance within the production system.
